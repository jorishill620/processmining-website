In four steps

What the process looks like.

Steps 1 to 3 take about four weeks together. After that, step 4 continues every month.

Week 1

Choose your process

We discuss where the pain is: purchase to pay, order to cash, claims or incidents. We agree which questions you want answered.

Week 1 to 2

We fetch the data

Our data engineers extract and transform the system data into a reliable event log. Every step is recorded and repeatable.

Week 3 to 4

We analyse

Deviations, lead times, rework and control breaches. You see what really happens, not what the handbook says.

Then every month

You act, we follow

A monthly session with concrete actions. The dashboard stays live, so you see straight away whether an improvement works.

What we ask of you

Very little. That is the whole idea.

You do not need a data expert in-house and you do not have to run a project.

  • Access to the data of the chosen process, through a connection or an extract. We handle the rest.
  • A contact person who can answer questions about the process.
  • One hour a month for the insight session with the people who need to act.
Where the work happens

In our environment, or on your site.

We adapt to your security requirements. The result is the same either way.

Insourcing: in our secured environment

The data goes to a shielded environment that only our dedicated team can access. We record who has access to what and arrange storage and deletion in a data processing agreement.

Co-sourcing: on your site

Does the data have to stay within your own environment? Then we work on your site or in your environment, together with your own team.

No long-term secondment. We transfer knowledge through our Academy, so that in time you can carry on independently if you wish.

What you get back

Something to act on, every month.

A live dashboard

Lead times, variants and deviations. Click from the big picture down to the individual case.

A short list of findings

The deviations with the most impact, with a suggestion for what to do about them. No hundred-page report.

Follow-up of actions

We track whether improvements work and show what has changed after three months.
