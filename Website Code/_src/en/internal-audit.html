Example controls

Where audit usually starts.

Payments without approval

Payments for which no approval is recorded, or where it was only given afterwards.

Changes to bank details

Suppliers whose bank account number was changed shortly before a payment.

Duplicate invoices

The same invoice, or nearly the same, booked or paid more than once.

Entries outside office hours

Manual journal entries at unusual times, or after the month-end close.
