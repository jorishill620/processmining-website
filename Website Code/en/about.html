About us

Process mining, done by people who do it every day.

processmining.nu is a service of Coney Solutions. We are a consultancy in data analysis, process mining and data-driven GRC, and we have been doing this work since 2005.

Why this subscription

Buying software is the easy part.

Many organisations buy process mining and then get no further: nobody fetches the data, models the process and turns the outcomes into action.

With this subscription we do that work. You get the insights, we take care of the rest, and the licences are in the price.
