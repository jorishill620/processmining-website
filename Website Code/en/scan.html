The process

Four weeks, four steps.

Week 1

Scoping

We choose the process and the questions you want answered, and agree access to the data.

Week 1 to 2

Fetching data

One extraction from your source system, converted into a reliable event log.

Week 2 to 3

Analysing

Process map, deviations, lead times, rework and control breaches.

Week 4

Presenting

A findings workshop with the people who need to act, plus the report and a demo dashboard.

What you get

Questions

About the scan.

What exactly does the scan cost?

From € 7,500 one-off, excl. VAT. The price depends on the number of source systems and the complexity of the process. You get the final price up front.

What if I take a subscription afterwards?

Within 60 days of the scan we deduct its cost from the onboarding and the first months of a Monitor or Continuous subscription.

What data do I need?

Access to the data of one process, for example purchase invoices or sales orders from your ERP. We handle the extraction and agree together what we need.

Do I have to install anything?

No. We use Process Science and Evidant in our own environment. You do not have to install anything.
