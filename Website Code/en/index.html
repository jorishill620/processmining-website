Questions

Good to know.

What do I need myself?

Access to the data of the process and a contact person who can turn the findings into action. We handle the extraction, the modelling and the analysis.

Will my data stay safe?

Yes. We work in a shielded environment, record who can access what, and agree storage and deletion in a data processing agreement.

Do I have to buy licences myself?

No. Use of Process Science and Evidant is part of your subscription. You receive one invoice.

How quickly will I see results?

After about four weeks we discuss the first findings. After that the picture refreshes every month.

Can I take it over myself later?

Yes. Through our Academy we train your team and transfer knowledge, so that in time you can carry on independently.
